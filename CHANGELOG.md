# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Per-line startup profiling for zsh and bash 5+, with nesting-aware attribution.
- `--whatif`: measured savings from switching off each hot line on a temporary copy of your zsh startup files.
- Fix hints and `--snippets` for nvm, pyenv-style managers, conda, compinit, brew, completion evals, oh-my-zsh, gcloud, sdkman/asdf/rvm, ssh-agent and generic `eval`.
- `--demo`, `--json`, `--no-source`, `--budget`.
